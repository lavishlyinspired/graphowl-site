Where the others are stronger

Relational databases

Transactions, throughput, tooling, and decades of operational knowledge. GraphOWL is not a system of record for transactions; it reads your databases.

Property-graph servers

Deep graph analytics, mature ecosystems, and full dialect coverage. GraphOWL traverses with bounds and supports a subset of Cypher.

RDF triple stores

Standards conformance and scale in the products built for it. GraphOWL states what it implements, dated, and publishes no benchmarks yet.

All of them

Maturity. GraphOWL is early access: the API may change before 1.0.

How we keep this honest

Every GraphOWL cell can be checked against the documentation, the dated standards page and the known limits. We publish no benchmark figures until we can publish the method that produced them. Last reviewed 13 October 2026.
